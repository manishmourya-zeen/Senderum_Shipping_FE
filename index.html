This Privacy Policy explains how Senderum Smart Checkout ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when merchants install and use the App on their Shopify store, and when their customers use the App during checkout. The App shows shipping rates, delivery options and pickup points at Shopify checkout, based on the shipping services and prices the merchant configures with their Senderum shipping account.

1

Information We Collect

Merchant and store information (stored)

When a merchant installs and uses the App, we collect and store:

  • Store details — the Shopify store domain, the store's primary language, and the access credentials Shopify provides when the App is installed.
  • Senderum account credentials — the credentials the merchant enters in the App settings to connect their Senderum shipping account.
  • Shipping configuration — the carriers, shipping services, prices, add-ons, delivery types, recommended carrier, location settings and other App settings the merchant configures.
  • Shopify shipping settings — a copy of the store's Shopify shipping profiles, locations, shipping zones (countries and regions), shipping methods and rate conditions, used to calculate rates consistently with the store's setup.

Customer checkout information (processed, not stored in our database)

When a customer reaches the shipping step at checkout, the App processes:

  • Shipping destination — Shopify sends the shipping address for the order (such as country, postal code, city and street address), together with the cart details relevant to shipping and the currency. Depending on the checkout, Shopify may also include the recipient's name and contact details.
  • Pickup-point search — when a customer chooses pickup delivery, the App uses the customer's shipping country, postal code, city and street to find nearby pickup points and show their approximate distance.
  • Delivery choices — the delivery type, carrier, shipping service and pickup point the customer selects.

We do not store this customer information in our database. We do not store customer profiles, customer records or order records, and we do not collect payment information.

2

How We Use Information

We use information only to provide the App:

  • To calculate and display shipping rates and delivery options at checkout.
  • To find nearby pickup points and show their distance from the customer's address.
  • To keep the App's rates aligned with the merchant's Shopify shipping settings and Senderum shipping account.
  • To connect the App securely to the merchant's store, keep it running, troubleshoot problems and provide support.

We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or build profiles of customers.

3

Shopify and Customer Data

  • The App accesses store data only through the permissions the merchant approves at installation, and only to provide its features.
  • The delivery choices a customer makes in the App — delivery type, carrier, service, selected pickup point and delivery address details — are saved by Shopify on the checkout and order. This information is stored in the merchant's Shopify store, not in our database.
  • We process customer information on behalf of the merchant, and only to provide shipping and pickup-point options.
4

Data Sharing

We share information only where needed to provide the App:

  • Shopify — to read store and shipping settings and to return shipping rates to checkout.
  • Senderum shipping services — to validate the merchant's Senderum account, synchronise the shipping configuration, and look up pickup points (using the customer's shipping country, postal code, city and street).
  • Geoapify — a geocoding service used to convert the customer's shipping address into map coordinates, so we can calculate the distance to nearby pickup points.

We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not share information with anyone else.

5

Data Storage and Retention

  • Merchant and store data is kept for as long as the App is installed on the store.
  • Customer checkout information is not stored in our database. It may be recorded in server logs used for troubleshooting.
6

Data Security

  • Information passing between Shopify, the App and the services described above is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Requests from Shopify are verified before they are processed.
  • Access to our systems is restricted to authorised personnel.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle.

7

Data Deletion and Privacy Rights

  • Uninstalling the App — when a merchant uninstalls the App, we delete that store's data from our database, including access credentials, the store record, shipping services and prices, and the synchronised shipping configuration.
  • Shopify privacy requests — we respond to Shopify's privacy requests. When Shopify asks us to erase a store's data, we delete all remaining data for that store. Because we do not store customer records, customer data and erasure requests usually involve no customer data held by us.
  • Customers — to access, correct or delete personal information, customers should contact the merchant they purchased from, who manages that information in Shopify.
  • Merchants — merchants can contact us about any privacy-related request using the details below.
8

Third-Party Services

The App relies on Shopify, Senderum shipping services and Geoapify to provide its features. Information processed by Shopify and Geoapify is also subject to their own privacy policies.

9

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
